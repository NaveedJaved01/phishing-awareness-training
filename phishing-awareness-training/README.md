## 📌 Introduction

Phishing is a common cybersecurity attack in which attackers use fraudulent emails, messages, and websites to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, and personal data.

This project focuses on **Phishing Awareness Training** to educate users about phishing attacks, fake websites, and social engineering techniques.

The objective is to help users recognize phishing attempts and follow cybersecurity best practices to stay safe online.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand phishing attacks and their working mechanisms.
- Learn how to recognize phishing emails.
- Identify fake and fraudulent websites.
- Understand social engineering tactics.
- Learn phishing prevention techniques.
- Explore real-world phishing examples.
- Test knowledge through interactive quizzes.

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. Introduction to Phishing
- What is phishing?
- Why attackers use phishing.
- How phishing affects individuals and organizations.

### 2. Types of Phishing
- Email Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Smishing (SMS Phishing)
- Vishing (Voice Phishing)
- Clone Phishing

### 3. How Phishing Works
1. Attackers select their targets.
2. They create fraudulent emails or messages.
3. Victims receive malicious links or attachments.
4. Victims are tricked into sharing sensitive information.
5. Attackers misuse stolen information.

### 4. Recognizing Phishing Emails
- Suspicious sender addresses.
- Urgent or threatening messages.
- Unexpected attachments.
- Suspicious hyperlinks.
- Spelling and grammatical mistakes.
- Requests for confidential information.

### 5. Identifying Fake Websites
- Check website URLs carefully.
- Look for suspicious domain names.
- Verify website certificates.
- Avoid unfamiliar login pages.
- Never share credentials on untrusted websites.

### 6. Social Engineering Techniques
- Creating urgency and fear.
- Impersonating trusted organizations.
- Offering fake rewards and prizes.
- Manipulating human emotions.
- Pretending to be technical support.

### 7. Real-World Examples
Educational examples demonstrating fraudulent emails, fake login pages, suspicious links, and common phishing scenarios.

### 8. Prevention Techniques
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Avoid clicking unknown links.
- Verify email senders.
- Keep software updated.
- Report suspicious messages.
- Use trusted security software.

### 9. Interactive Quiz
A knowledge assessment to help users recognize phishing emails, fake websites, and suspicious online activities.
